Notion 2.0 Formulas FAQ

Why did Notion introduce Formulas 2.0?

Legacy Notion formulas required deeply nested if(cond, val, if(cond, val, ...)) statements that were virtually unreadable. Formulas 2.0 brings functional programming: let() variables, multi-branch ifs(), and array operations.

How does the let() function work?

let(name, value, expression) binds a calculation to a temporary variable name. You can reference name multiple times throughout your formula without recalculating it.

Are my formulas private?

Yes. The entire linter and sandbox evaluator run locally in your browser with zero network requests.

Can I copy formulas directly into Notion?

Yes. Clicking "Copy Formula" copies the exact formatted or minified string directly to your clipboard, ready to paste into any Notion database formula property.
